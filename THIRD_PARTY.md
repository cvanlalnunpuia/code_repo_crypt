# Third-party resources

The upstream IHOP implementation is available at https://github.com/simon-oya/ihop-code. The experiments used commit `10631a10e3ec461ad01d38fc74f7933d31062ba5`. Its source and processed datasets are excluded from this release. Acquire them from the upstream project under its applicable terms.

WMT23 IndicNE-Corp, FLORES-200 and Enron corpus text are excluded. Dataset locations and acquisition identifiers are provided in the archive. Access and redistribution remain subject to their respective terms.

The official Springer Nature template is available from https://www.springernature.com/gp/authors/campaigns/latex-author-support. Original class and bibliography files are excluded from the public archive. The setup script obtains the required publisher files and checks their hashes. Their existing licence notices remain unchanged.

Python dependencies retain their own licences. No project-wide licence was assigned by this release.
